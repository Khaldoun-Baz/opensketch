# Code of Conduct

OpenSketch is a welcoming project for people of every background and
experience level, from first-time contributors to CAD experts.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient; assume good intent.
- Give constructive, specific feedback on ideas and code, not on people.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory or sexualized language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors for repeated or serious violations.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through their GitHub profile
(@Khaldoun-Baz). Reports are handled confidentially.
